What makes a happy life?

A research agent that answers your questions about happiness with real survey data: 75,699 Americans interviewed since 1972 (General Social Survey), 169 countries scored yearly by the Gallup World Poll (World Happiness Report), and moment-by-moment feelings people reported during their daily activities (American Time Use Survey Well-Being Module). Built for the curious: students, journalists, and anyone who wonders whether money, marriage, or friends actually move the needle.
