    how it works

    1. 01

      you ask

      Anything about happiness, wellbeing or life satisfaction. If the question is too vague, or the data can't cover it, the desk says so and suggests sharper ones.

    2. 02

      the desk digs

      A lead splits your question into tasks. Up to six researchers work them at the same time, each writing SQL against the survey data. You can watch every query as it runs.

    3. 03

      a story gets filed

      A writer turns the findings into a short piece: the answer first, then charts, sources on every claim, and what the data can't tell you. It takes two to four minutes.
